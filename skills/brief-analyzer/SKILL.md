# Brief Analyzer

## Process

1. Classify the project type and viewing context.
2. Extract must-say information, optional information, forbidden content, and missing details. Separate sourced facts, proposed assumptions, and decisions requiring the user; never invent brand claims, specifications, deadlines, or budgets.
3. Identify audience, business goal, communication goal, emotional goal, and delivery constraints.
4. Convert vague wording into actionable creative requirements.
