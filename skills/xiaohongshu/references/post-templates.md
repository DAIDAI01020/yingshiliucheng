# Xiaohongshu Post Templates

## Standard One-Shot Caption

```text
标题：
一日一镜 001｜雨夜出租车后座

正文：
如果孤独是一场夜戏，我会把它放在出租车后座。

人物不需要哭，也不需要说话，只要靠着车窗，看城市的灯从脸上滑过去。

这个镜头的重点不是“难过”，而是雨水、玻璃、霓虹和沉默一起把情绪拍出来。

镜头设定：50mm 中近景，人物靠窗，脸只被一半光照亮。
光线：车内暗光 + 街边霓虹 + 雨水反射。
适合拍成：短片开场 / 情绪转场 / 失恋后独处片段。

#一日一镜 #AI电影感 #影视视觉开发 #电影镜头 #AI分镜
```

## Scene Design Board

Use this when the post is about AI film scene design, production design, art direction, interior scene concepts, short-film locations, commercial set design, or save-worthy space ideas.

```text
Image 1: hero scene
  A wide or medium-wide view that clearly shows the space and story mood.

Image 2: camera angle / blocking
  Show where a person could stand, sit, enter, wait, hide, or interact with the set.

Image 3: material / prop detail
  Show the objects, wall texture, window, table, lamp, product, fabric, dust, stains, or traces that make the space believable.

Image 4 optional: lighting-camera plan card
  Explain light source, lens, camera height, and direction.

Image 5 optional: scene design note card
  Explain story function, palette, materials, props, and shoot value.
```

## Prompt Quality Gate

```text
Before image generation, reject prompts that only say:
"cinematic, high quality, movie still, emotional, beautiful lighting".

Accept prompts only when they include:
1. dramatic beat
2. exact visible frame
3. actor blocking
4. camera height / lens / shot size
5. motivated light source
6. production design details
7. material texture and life traces
8. AI failure controls
```

## Bad vs Good Prompt Example

Bad:

```text
A lonely person in a taxi on a rainy night, cinematic, high quality, emotional lighting, movie still.
```

Good:

```text
Create a vertical 4:5 director-grade cinematic still for a Xiaohongshu "one shot per day" film visual diary.
Dramatic beat: a passenger has just ended a conversation and is delaying the moment of going home.
Visible frame: an adult East Asian passenger sits in the back seat of a taxi beside a rain-streaked side window; the city outside is reduced to blurred red brake lights and cyan storefront reflections; the opposite seat edge is a dark foreground shape.
Blocking: the passenger leans slightly toward the window, shoulders closed, gaze outside instead of at camera, one hand loose near the coat seam, no crying and no posed sadness.
Scene and production design: realistic Chinese city taxi interior at midnight, worn black seat fabric, faint water mark near the door, plastic partition edge, small scratches on glass, wet asphalt reflections outside.
Camera grammar: 50mm medium close-up from the opposite rear seat, camera at seated eye level, subject off-center, shallow depth of field, window reflection crossing only part of the face, negative space toward the dark window.
Lighting logic: dashboard spill from below, passing neon reflected by wet glass, soft cyan and amber light sliding across one side of the face, the other side held in deep shadow, no beauty key light.
Color and exposure: muted cool blacks, restrained red and amber highlights, protected skin tones, natural low-light exposure, no fake HDR.
Texture: real skin texture, fabric wrinkles, raindrops on glass, small dust on the door trim, subtle film grain.
Performance state: quiet restraint, tired eyes, the emotion stays under the surface.
Constraints: no text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark, no beauty smoothing, no plastic AI skin, no generic cyberpunk, no distorted hands/faces, no random extra people.
Aspect ratio: vertical 4:5.
```

## Three Prompt Roles

```text
cover prompt:
The strongest single film still, readable as a Xiaohongshu cover.

close-up prompt:
A closer emotional continuation from the same scene, usually tighter lens and shallower depth of field.

empty/detail prompt:
No obvious performance beat; use space, light, prop, rain, fabric, wall, glass, or leftover trace to end the post.
```
