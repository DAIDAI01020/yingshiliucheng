## Rules

- Prioritize conflict, reversal, and emotional clarity.
- Keep the concept easy to turn into scenes.
- Do not overbuild worldbuilding if the user needs quick production.
- For AI workflows, flag expensive or unstable elements: crowds, battles, complex VFX, exact text, many locations.
