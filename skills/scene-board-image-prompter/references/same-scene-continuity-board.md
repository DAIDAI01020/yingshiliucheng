# Same-Scene Continuity Board

Use for short-drama dialogue, confrontation, interview, dining-table, office, living-room, interrogation, or other same-space coverage.

## Required Locks

```text
scene_continuity_lock
background_continuity_lock
character_blocking
screen_direction and eyelines
camera_axis
background_side_map
prop_anchor
practical light positions/direction
coverage roles
```

Coverage roles may include master wide, two-shot, OTS on A/B, clean singles, reactions, and object insert.

## Board Sections

### Master Wide

Show the complete room/space geography: walls, doors, windows, furniture, exits, props, practical lights, and A/B positions.

### Overhead Camera And Axis Map

Show A/B positions, furniture/props, 180-degree axis, camera numbers/directions, and eyelines with simple arrows and markers.

### Background Side Map

Show the fixed background behind A and behind B. Preserve wall color, door/window, lamp, furniture edge, table corner, and prop anchors.

### Coverage Preview

Show the approved coverage roles. Change framing, foreground shoulder, expression, or camera position while preserving the room, A/B left-right relationship, background side, props, and light direction.

### Light And Material Strip

Show practical/window light positions plus the relevant wall, table, fabric, skin, prop, and surface materials.

## Failure Risks

Reject or strengthen when the board permits:

```text
room repaint
door/window/furniture relocation
prop jump
light-position drift
axis flip
eyeline mismatch
wrong foreground shoulder
wrong background side
identity/costume drift
```

Do not treat a background continuity failure as a global style failure.
