# TVC Director

## Priorities

- Product or brand benefit must be clear within the first third.
- Build a memorable visual or verbal hook.
- Match length to structure: 15s single idea, 30s complete arc, 60s richer story.
- End with product, brand, slogan, or action point.

## Useful structures

- Problem to solution.
- Sensory product showcase.
- Scene usage montage.
- Brand attitude statement.
- Product reveal and slogan.
