# Reference Style Translation Guide

Use this when the user names a reference such as Apple keynote, Nike ad, CCTV program package, Mercedes commercial, luxury fashion film, or another recognizable visual source.

## Rule

Translate references into methods. Do not copy exact shots, logos, slogans, UI layouts, stage design, character styling, or distinctive protected compositions.

## Output

| 参考对象 | 可借鉴方法 | 转译为本项目 | 避免照搬 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Then provide:

- 推荐主风格
- 可执行视觉关键词
- 可用于AI出图的摄影语言
- 不建议方向

## Common references

### Apple keynote / Apple product launch

Borrow:

- Minimal product reveal.
- Black, white, gray, and restrained accent color.
- Precise rim light and product contour light.
- Slow push-in, clean stage space, calm pacing.
- Large negative space and concise typography.
- Product as the hero, not decorative effects.

Translate into:

- 极简科技发布会质感。
- 产品或主题被像舞台主角一样揭示。
- 干净空间、克制镜头、精准光线、少量文字。

Avoid:

- Copying Apple UI, stage, slogans, product framing, or exact keynote composition.

### Nike-style sports ad

Borrow:

- Human drive, sweat, impact, speed, breath, rhythm.
- Low-angle hero shots, handheld energy, hard contrast.
- Short slogans, strong beat, cut-on-action.

Translate into:

- 运动精神、人物爆发力、强节奏剪辑、身体细节。

Avoid:

- Copying Nike slogans, campaigns, athlete styling, or exact ad scenes.

### CCTV / major TV program package

Borrow:

- Clear information hierarchy.
- Title reveal, stage light, graphic rhythm, authoritative polish.
- Faces, hosts, program name, broadcast time, and channel information readability.

Translate into:

- 高能电视包装、清晰栏目识别、舞台灯光、强节奏字幕包装。

Avoid:

- Copying exact channel graphics, logo systems, opening title design, or proprietary animation language.

### Mercedes / luxury automotive commercial

Borrow:

- Controlled reflections, premium surfaces, road scale, low-angle tracking.
- Night city, tunnel light, mountain road, rain reflection, elegant speed.

Translate into:

- 高级汽车广告质感、车身线条、速度与空间、克制豪华。

Avoid:

- Copying exact model shots, brand grille treatment, slogan, or ad composition.

### Luxury fashion film

Borrow:

- Texture, silhouette, editorial pose, slow motion, moody light.
- Architectural space, fabric movement, skin and material detail.

Translate into:

- 高级时尚影像、人物轮廓、材质触感、情绪化空间。

Avoid:

- Copying named brand campaigns, styling, logo usage, or runway designs.
