# Visual Language Guide

## Style inference matrix

When a script or storyboard has no explicit style, infer the most suitable main style before making prompts.

| 稿件信号 | 推荐主风格 | 摄影/视觉倾向 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 产品卖点清晰、消费场景、品牌slogan | 高端商业电影感 | ARRI Alexa 35, Zeiss Supreme Prime, clean premium lighting |
| 人物故事、城市温度、价值观、情绪旁白 | 情绪叙事电影感 | Alexa Mini LF or Sony Venice 2, Cooke warmth, soft contrast |
| 真实人物、社会观察、生活细节 | 纪实人文质感 | Blackmagic URSA Cine, natural light, handheld restraint |
| 科技、汽车、新能源、数字化、未来 | 科技未来视觉 | Sony Venice 2 or RED V-Raptor, glass/metal, controlled neon |
| 综艺、节目预告、赛制、冲突、高能 | 节目包装高能感 | Angenieux Optimo, dynamic zoom, bold graphic lighting |
| 发布会、盛典、论坛、主题揭幕 | 仪式开场大片感 | Alexa Mini LF, anamorphic scale, volumetric light, grand reveal |
| LED、舞美、沉浸、宽屏、空间视觉 | 沉浸式大屏视觉 | large-scale composition, center-safe subject, low text density |

Always include:

- 推荐主风格
- 推荐理由
- 1-2个备选风格
- 不建议方向

## Common tones

- 高端商业：克制构图、精致光影、浅景深、干净空间、慢速推进、产品高光。
- 科技未来：冷暖对比、发光界面、数据流、机械结构、玻璃金属、空间纵深。
- 年轻潮流：高饱和点缀、快切、手持或运动镜头、街头材质、字幕节奏强。
- 纪实人文：自然光、生活场景、真实人物、轻微颗粒、温暖色温、观察式镜头。
- 电视节目感：强节奏包装、人物看点、舞台灯光、冲突悬念、信息层级清晰。
- 大屏沉浸：宏观空间、中心主体、低文字密度、大面积运动、强视觉冲击。

## Camera language

- Establishing shot: 交代空间、城市、舞台或品牌世界观。
- Product hero shot: 突出产品轮廓、材质、功能点和记忆点。
- Macro shot: 展示细节、工艺、材料、触感。
- Tracking shot: 带出动线、体验、空间穿梭和情绪推进。
- Push-in: 强调信息揭示、情绪靠近、品牌主张落点。
- Overhead or top shot: 展示秩序、版图、阵列、舞台调度。

## Cinematography references for AI image prompts

Use these as visual-language references in prompts:

- ARRI Alexa 35: high-end TVC, soft highlight roll-off, natural skin, premium controlled color.
- ARRI Alexa Mini LF: large-format cinematic depth, elegant brand film, shallow focus, spacious composition.
- Sony Venice 2: modern luxury, clean low-light, polished commercial image, smooth skin tone.
- RED V-Raptor: crisp high-resolution detail, bold contrast, technology, action, product precision.
- Blackmagic URSA Cine: grounded cinema texture, documentary realism, less glossy and more human.

Lens families:

- Zeiss Supreme Prime: clean, sharp, modern, luxury commercial.
- Cooke S4/i: warm skin tone, gentle contrast, emotional human scenes.
- Panavision anamorphic: widescreen flare, oval bokeh, epic scale.
- Angenieux Optimo zoom: live event, sports, broadcast, program promo energy.
- 100mm macro: product texture, material, craftsmanship, food, beauty.

When generating multiple options for a storyboard frame, vary the cinematography meaningfully:

- Option A: commercial clarity and premium product readability.
- Option B: emotional mood and atmospheric storytelling.
- Option C: bold art direction, surreal scale, or broadcast/package energy.

## Motion design

- Use motion to reveal information, not just decorate.
- For program promos, sync motion with beats, punchlines, conflict points, or reveal moments.
- For LED screens, avoid excessive tiny particles, dense text, and fast edge-to-edge motion that may cause现场眩晕.

## Composition

- Keep brand/product/host/title readable in the first visual impression.
- Keep text center-safe for LED, stage, and broadcast lower-third risks.
- Use negative space intentionally for subtitle, logo, host, or product reveal.
