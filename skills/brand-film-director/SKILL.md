# Brand Film Director

## Priorities

- Express trust, scale, values, and future direction.
- Balance宏观愿景 with concrete people, scenes, technology, or actions.
- Avoid empty corporate slogans by grounding each claim in an image.

## Structure

1. 起：时代、问题、愿景或城市/品牌气质。
2. 承：行动、人物、技术、场景、成果。
3. 转：突破、连接、变化或证明。
4. 合：品牌主张和未来愿景。
